01 · Booking App · Component Design

One card, four lifecycle states.

The expense card had to communicate where each expense sits in its journey — from AI capture to claimed. Every state maps to a rule users can feel in the flow.

To be reviewed

Default after AI capture. The entry exists but needs a human glance before it can travel further.

Reviewed

Vendor, amount and category confirmed — the gate that makes an expense trustworthy enough to claim.

Exportable

Reviewed expenses become selectable for a claimable report — the status maps to the export rule.

Disabled

Exported or invalid entries are visually retired — preventing double-claims without deleting history.

01 · Booking App · Flow Decision

Two flows prototyped, one deliberate comparison.

Flow 1 — Review first

Capture → AI scan → review required → lists → export

Trade-off: guarantees clean data early, but interrupts users mid-capture — high friction at the worst moment: standing at a counter, receipt in hand.

Flow 2 — Review later · Shipped

Capture → AI scan → lists → review optional before export

Trade-off: users capture and move on; review is deferred to export time, when they're focused and motivated. Lower friction, better error recovery.

The judgment callCompared on friction, cognitive load, and error recovery — nobody reviews a receipt while a queue forms behind them.
